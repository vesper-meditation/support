Privacy Policy

Vesper · Last updated 11 October 2026

Vesper writes a guided meditation around whatever you tell it. That means you sometimes type something private. This page explains plainly what we collect, where it goes, and how to get rid of it.

The short version. We collect what we need to run the app and nothing else. We use no analytics, no advertising, and no tracking SDKs. We do not sell your data, and we never use what you write to train anyone's AI models.

What we collect

DataWhy
Email addressTo create and sign you into your account.
First nameSo the app can address you by name.
Date of birthTo determine your zodiac sign for the daily reading. Nothing else.
What you write before a sessionIt is the input the meditation is written from.
Reflections you save after a sessionTo show you your own history.
Preferences — voice, background sound, session length, time zoneTo keep the app set up the way you like it, and to send the morning reading at the right local hour.
Notification tokenOnly if you enable notifications, so we can deliver them.
Subscription statusTo unlock the app for subscribers.

We do not collect your location, your contacts, your browsing or search history, your payment details, or any advertising identifier.

Who we share it with

We use a small number of service providers to run Vesper. They process data on our instructions and may not use it for their own purposes.

ProviderWhat it receives
SupabaseHosts our database and accounts: your email, name, date of birth, preferences, sessions and reflections.
AnthropicReceives the text you write, in order to compose the meditation script.
ElevenLabsReceives the finished script text, in order to synthesize the voice audio.
Cloudflare / Supabase StorageStores the generated audio files.
UpstashRate limiting. Receives no personal content.
RevenueCat & AppleHandle the subscription. Apple processes the payment — we never see your card details.

Your session text is sent to Anthropic and ElevenLabs purely to generate that session. It is not used to train their models.

How long we keep it

  • Your account and preferences: until you ask us to delete them.
  • Sessions: we keep only your most recent sessions — older ones are pruned automatically.
  • Generated audio: deleted on a rolling schedule once it is no longer needed.

Your choices

  • Delete everything. Email us from your account address and we will permanently delete your account and all associated data within 30 days.
  • Get a copy. Ask and we will send you what we hold.
  • Correct it. Name, date of birth and preferences are editable in the app.
  • Turn off notifications. In the app's settings, or in iOS Settings.

If you are in the EU or UK, these rights are yours under the GDPR. If you are in California, the CCPA applies — note that we do not sell or share personal information as those laws define it.

Children

Vesper is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their data. If you believe a child has created an account, email us and we will remove it.

Security

Data is encrypted in transit and at rest by our providers, and access to our systems is limited to what is needed to operate the app. No service can promise perfect security, but we take it seriously.

Changes

If this policy changes materially we will update the date above and note the change in the app.

Contact
