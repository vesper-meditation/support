Common questions

How do I cancel my subscription?

Subscriptions are billed by Apple, not by us, so they're cancelled from your device: open Settings → tap your name → Subscriptions → Vesper → Cancel Subscription. You keep access until the end of the period you've already paid for.

How do I get a refund?

Refunds are handled by Apple. Request one at reportaproblem.apple.com. If something went wrong on our side, email us and we'll help you make the case.

I paid but the app says I'm not subscribed.

Open Settings → Subscription in Vesper and tap Restore Purchases. Make sure you're signed in with the same Apple ID used for the purchase. If it still doesn't appear, email us with the date of purchase and we'll sort it out.

How does the free trial work?

The monthly plan includes a 7-day free trial. Apple allows one trial per Apple ID. Cancel any time before the trial ends and you won't be charged.

A session failed to generate.

Sessions are created on our servers, so a connection is required. If one fails, try again — and if it keeps happening, email us with the rough time it occurred so we can find it in our logs.

Can I use Vesper offline?

Not yet. Generating a meditation needs a network connection.

How do I delete my account and my data?

Email us from the address on your account and we'll permanently delete your account and everything attached to it — your sessions, what you wrote, and your preferences — within 30 days.

Is Vesper a medical service?

No. Vesper supports well-being but is not a substitute for professional care, and it does not diagnose or treat any condition. The daily reading is written for reflection, not prediction.

If you're in crisis, please contact your local emergency services. In the US you can call or text 988 to reach the Suicide & Crisis Lifeline.
